How ToolStack handles data.

1) Data we collect

Tool settings: Some tools may save your preferences (like timer minutes) in your browser using localStorage. This stays on your device.

Analytics: We may use analytics to understand usage (pages visited, device type, approximate location). Analytics providers may set cookies.

Advertising (future): If ads are enabled, ad partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show relevant ads and measure performance.

2) Cookies
Cookies may be used by analytics/advertising providers. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work as expected.
3) Third-party services
Third-party services (analytics/ads) may collect information according to their own policies.
4) Contact
Questions? Contact us via the Contact page.
