Reading Time Calculator

Estimate reading time based on word count and reading speed.

WPM defaults: silent ~200, aloud ~130. Estimates only.
Explanation

Estimates reading time from a word count and reading speed.

  • Words are counted from your pasted text.
  • Reading time: minutes = words ÷ WPM and the remainder becomes seconds.
  • Typical web reading speeds are ~200–250 WPM (varies by language and difficulty).
