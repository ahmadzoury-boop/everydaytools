Case Converter

Convert text casing quickly. Includes cleaning spaces and swapping input/output.

Tip: Use “Clean spaces” to remove extra spaces and tidy lines.
Explanation

Converts the same text into different letter cases.

  • Uppercase / lowercase are straightforward character transforms.
  • Title Case capitalizes the first letter of each word (best for headings).
  • Sentence case capitalizes the first letter of each sentence (best for paragraphs).
