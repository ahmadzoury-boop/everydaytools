Explanation

This tool compares your date of birth to an “as of” date and returns your age.

  • Counts calendar differences (years → months → days), so it handles different month lengths and leap years.
  • Total days is computed from the exact date difference (not “30 days per month”).
  • Useful for forms, HR, insurance, and checking age on a specific date.
