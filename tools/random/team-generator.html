Team Generator

Paste names, choose number of teams, then generate fair random teams (Fisher–Yates shuffle).

Explanation

Splits a list of names into random teams.

  • The list is shuffled (randomized order), then divided evenly into the number of teams.
  • If the players don’t divide perfectly, extra players are distributed one-by-one to the first teams.
