How this VAT calculator works

This tool helps you quickly work with VAT using three modes:

  • Add VAT: Start with a net price and add VAT.
  • Remove VAT: Start with a gross price and remove VAT.
  • Custom VAT: Use your own VAT percentage for any region.

Choose a country preset to auto-fill a common VAT rate, or select “Custom rate…” to enter a specific percentage. The calculator then shows the VAT amount, the price without VAT, and the total price including VAT.

Common VAT questions

What is VAT?

VAT (Value Added Tax) is a consumption tax added to most goods and services. Businesses often need to calculate prices with and without VAT for invoices, quotes, and accounting.

Is this calculator suitable for my country?

You can use this VAT calculator for any country by selecting a preset or entering a custom rate. Always confirm current VAT rules with your local tax authority.
