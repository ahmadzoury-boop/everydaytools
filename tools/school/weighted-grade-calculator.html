Explanation

Calculates your weighted average grade from multiple components.

  • Each row contributes: contribution = weight% × score% ÷ 100.
  • Overall grade (if weights sum to 100): total = Σ(weight×score) ÷ 100.
  • If weights don’t sum to 100, the tool also shows a normalized weighted average.
  • Optional “target grade” can show the needed average on remaining weight.
