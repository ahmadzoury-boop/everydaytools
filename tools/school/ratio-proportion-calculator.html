Explanation

Works with ratios in 3 modes: simplify, scale, and solve for x.

  • Simplify: divide both sides by their GCD (greatest common divisor).
  • Scale: multiply both sides by the same factor to resize the ratio.
  • Solve: for a:b = c:x → x = (b×c) ÷ a.
