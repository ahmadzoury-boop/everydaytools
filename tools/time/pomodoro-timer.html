How it works
Work in focused sessions, then take a short break. After a set number of focus sessions, take a longer break. This tool saves your settings locally in your browser.
Explanation

Pomodoro uses focused work blocks with scheduled breaks.

  • Cycle: Focus → Short break, and after N focus sessions → Long break.
  • Settings are stored in your browser (local storage) so they persist.
  • Keyboard shortcuts let you control it hands-free.
