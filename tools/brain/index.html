🧠 Daily Brain

A daily brain workout with short, smart challenges designed to improve memory, focus, and logical thinking.

Every day resets at midnight (UTC). Complete challenges, build streaks, and track your progress over time.

How it works:
  • Each day has 3 difficulty levels: Easy → Medium → Hard
  • Complete Easy to unlock Medium, Medium to unlock Hard
  • Your score and streak are saved on this device
  • The challenge resets every day at 00:00 UTC
Show:

🔥 Your streak increases when you complete Easy + Medium on the same day.

Want a reminder?
You can optionally subscribe to receive the daily brain challenge by email. No spam, no ads — just one short challenge per day.
